Home widgets, Quick controls, camera and mic indicators, and one click updates.

## New

* **Home widgets.** A large clock card, a battery ring, the music you paused (or your notifications, or Wi-Fi), and a dock of 4 to 6 quick apps you choose in Settings › Personalization › Home. Each app sits on a tile tinted with its own colour.
* **Quick controls.** Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, island Do not disturb and dark mode toggles, rounded volume and brightness sliders, speaker and microphone switching, mic mute, and shortcuts, all without opening Windows Settings.
* **Camera and microphone indicators.** A green dot inside the island while an app uses the camera, an orange one while an app records from a real, unmuted microphone. Muted mics and virtual audio cables are ignored.
* **Several players at once.** Other apps playing in the background appear under the player as "Also playing"; click one and it rises into the main spot.
* **Clock app stopwatch.** A stopwatch running in the Windows Clock app shows in the island too. Island timers and the stopwatch start from the right click menu.
* **One UI style notifications.** One Notifications tab, conversations first, everything else below.
* **Updates in one click.** The island checks GitHub for new versions, shows a banner, and installs from Settings › Updates. From this version on you never need to download an installer by hand.

## Better

* Colourful tab icons, and the island reopens on the tab you left (on Music whenever something is playing).
* Settings redesigned: one accent colour, coloured page icons, Overview tiles, full height, and the tab row stays visible.
* Smooth hover and press animations on every button, tile and row.
* The tucked bar reacts as you approach: it swells, fills while you rest on it, then opens.
* Skipping a song keeps the player open while the app loads the next track, with an instant nudge so every press feels answered.
* No more jitter when the island shrinks from a big card to a small one.

## Install

Download `DynamicIsland-Setup-2.0.0-x64.exe` below (or `arm64` for ARM PCs) and run it. It upgrades 1.0 in place and keeps your settings.
